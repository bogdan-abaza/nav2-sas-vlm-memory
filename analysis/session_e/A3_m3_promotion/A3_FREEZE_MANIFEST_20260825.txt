SENSITIVITY
  Consistency is the discriminating parameter, at minimum frequency 3:
    <= 0.50      all three promote
    0.51 - 0.75  quiet_corner and wet_gloves promote
    0.76 - 1.00  only wet_gloves promotes          <- frozen 0.80 sits on this plateau
  quiet_corner is 0.05 below the threshold; a threshold of exactly 0.75 would promote it.
  Frequency is weakly identified: all three candidates have frequency 4, so minimum
  frequency 1, 2, 3 and 4 give the same result, while 5 or more rejects everything.
  Session E therefore does NOT empirically justify minimum frequency = 3.

INTERPRETATION GUARDRAIL
  The gate measures repeated-output STABILITY, not semantic correctness. A3 does not
  assess ground-truth correctness of the promoted node. The three candidates are
  controlled induction cases, not a sample supporting a universally optimal threshold.
  The ">= 1 L3b_vlm" condition is held fixed and is not ablated.
  Present A3 as post-hoc robustness characterization of a PRE-frozen rule, never as
  data-driven threshold selection.
